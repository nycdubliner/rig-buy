The Strategy: Why OCuLink?

Your MSI MEG X570 Unify lacks a Thunderbolt header, making standard eGPU enclosures unstable. However, it features three M.2 PCIe 4.0 x4 slots. We will convert the 3rd slot into an external 64Gbps port—twice the bandwidth of Thunderbolt 3.

  • No Bottleneck: OCuLink 4.0 provides a native PCIe link, ensuring your 3rd GPU performs at ~95% of its internal speed.
  • Pure AMD: Adding a third 7800 XT keeps your ROCm drivers unified and ensures seamless layer splitting in llama.cpp.

Implementation Notes

Physical Modification: You will need to route the OCuLink cable out of your Lian Li 216 case. This is easily done by removing a PCIe slot bracket or using the rear grommets.

BIOS Configuration

  1. Install the M.2 adapter into the bottom-most slot (M2_3).
  2. In BIOS, set the PCIe speed for that slot to Gen 4 (do not leave on Auto).
  3. Enable Above 4G Decoding and Re-size BAR Support.

Software Setup

ROCm will automatically detect the 3rd card. When running llama.cpp, you can now use the -ngl flag to offload 100% of a 70B model's layers across the 48GB VRAM pool.
