The Strategy: Why OCuLink?

Your MSI MEG X570 Unify lacks a Thunderbolt header, making standard eGPU enclosures unstable. However, it features three M.2 PCIe 4.0 x4 slots. We will convert the 3rd slot into an external 64Gbps port—twice the bandwidth of Thunderbolt 3.

  • No Bottleneck: OCuLink 4.0 provides a native PCIe link, ensuring your 3rd GPU performs at ~95% of its internal speed.
  • Pure AMD: Adding a third 7800 XT keeps your ROCm drivers unified and ensures seamless layer splitting in llama.cpp.

The "40GB Cliff" & Context Windows

To run a 70B model (like Llama-3 70B) at a highly accurate 4-bit quantization entirely in VRAM, you need to cross the ~40GB VRAM threshold. If you fall short, the model spills over into slow system DDR4 RAM, and generation speed plummets to ~1-2 tokens/sec.

The Context Ceiling: Hitting 40GB is only half the battle. You also need VRAM headroom for your "Context Window" (the conversation history and documents you feed the AI).

The 3rd GPU Dilemma: Speed vs. Capacity

Adding a third GPU to your current 32GB pool presents a critical choice:

  • Setup A: Add RTX 3080 10GB (~€350) = 42GB Total.
    The Speed Play: The 3080 has a massive 760 GB/s bandwidth and Tensor Cores. It will generate text very quickly. However, with only 42GB total, you have just ~2GB of headroom. You will hit Out-Of-Memory (OOM) errors on long documents.
  • Setup B: Add RX 7600 XT 16GB (~€330) = 48GB Total.
    The Capacity Play: The 7600 XT is much slower (288 GB/s), which will throttle your overall generation speed slightly. But, it gives you ~8GB of headroom. You can comfortably load massive 32k+ token contexts without crashing.

Verdict: If you prioritize snappy, short-form chat, the used RTX 3080 10GB is an incredible "Bang for Buck" speed upgrade. If you prioritize analyzing massive documents or coding workflows with long histories, prioritize the 16GB capacity of the 7600 XT or save up for a 3090.

Alternative Phase 3: The "Dual XTX" Internal Swap

If you prefer to avoid the physical complexities of an external OCuLink dock, there is a cleaner path to 48GB VRAM: Selling your 2x 7800 XTs and buying 2x RX 7900 XTX 24GB cards.

  • The Market Reality: Local classifieds (like Facebook Marketplace or Adverts.ie) occasionally list used 7900 XTXs for as low as €700. Because they lack the "NVIDIA Tax," they are often significantly cheaper than a used RTX 3090.
  • The Math: Sell 2x 7800 XT (+€800). Buy 2x 7900 XTX (-€1400). Net cost to reach 48GB VRAM is only ~€600. This makes it cheaper than adding a 3rd external card.
  • Rig Building Checks: Two 7900 XTXs pull massive power. A 1000W PSU is cutting it extremely close; you may need to strictly undervolt them.

The "Vertical Stack" Solution

On the MSI X570 Unify, a traditional horizontal setup will thermally choke the top card. The Lian Li LANCOOL 216 offers a unique escape route through its rotatable PCIe panel.

Opportunities

  • 7 Vertical Slots: Rotating the rear panel turns the 7 horizontal slots into 7 vertical slots.
  • The 20mm Gap: Mounting GPU 1 in slots 1-3 and GPU 2 in slots 5-7 leaves Slot 4 (20.3mm) as a dedicated air gap.
  • Thermal Bypass: Cold air from the front 160mm fans can reach the fans of both cards directly.

Challenges & DIY Hurdles

  • Mounting Stability: The shroud only has screw holes for 1 riser base. The 2nd card requires DIY standoffs or heavy-duty mounting tape to secure the riser.
  • Slot Thickness: At least one card MUST be <3 slots (e.g. Reference 51mm). See the 7900 XTX Clearance Master List for a full comparison of AIB model widths. A 3.5-slot AIB card will close the gap to <10mm, causing turbulence.
  • Cable Cost: €100 total. Requires 2x high-quality PCIe 4.0 risers (LinkUp recommended).
  • Glass Proximity: Outer card sits ~35mm from the glass. High RPM front intake is mandatory to prevent heat soak.

Implementation Notes

Physical Modification: You will need to route the OCuLink cable out of your Lian Li 216 case. This is easily done by removing a PCIe slot bracket or using the rear grommets.

BIOS Configuration

  1. Install the M.2 adapter into the bottom-most slot (M2_3).
  2. In BIOS, set the PCIe speed for that slot to Gen 4 (do not leave on Auto).
  3. Enable Above 4G Decoding and Re-size BAR Support.

Software Setup

ROCm will automatically detect the 3rd card. When running llama.cpp, you can now use the -ngl flag to offload 100% of a 70B model's layers across the 48GB VRAM pool.
