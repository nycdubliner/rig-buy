Physical Assembly Guide: Build Day

Finalizing the Dual RX 7800 XT AI Workstation on the MSI MEG X570 Unify platform.

← Back to Main Build Quote

1 Storage: NVMe SSD

Install the WD Blue SN5100 before the GPUs obstruct access to the motherboard surface.

  • Slot: Use M2_1 (the topmost slot, located between the CPU socket and the first PCIe slot).
  • Rationale: This slot is wired directly to the Ryzen 5900X's Gen4 lanes, ensuring the fastest boot and model loading times.
  • Installation: Unscrew the "Frozr" heatsink, insert the drive at a 30-degree angle, press down, and secure the heatsink back over the drive.

2 Dual-GPU Placement (The x8/x8 Split)

To run two GPUs for AI, you must bypass the chipset and use the CPU-connected lanes.

  • Primary Slot (PCI_E1 - Top): Install the XFX Speedster QICK 319.
  • Secondary Slot (PCI_E3 - Third Slot Down): Install the Gigabyte Gaming OC.
CRITICAL: Do NOT use the middle slot (PCI_E2). On the MSI Unify, only slots 1 and 3 support the x8/x8 bifurcation required for dual-GPU AI workloads.
Airflow Check: The LANCOOL 216 should be in Air Cooling Mode (check the rear PCIe bracket position) to maximize the gap between the bottom GPU and the PSU shroud.

3 Motherboard Power

  • 24-Pin ATX: Standard connection on the right edge of the board.
  • CPU Power (8+8 Pin): Located at the top left. The MSI Unify has two 8-pin headers.
  • Action: Use two separate 4+4/8-pin CPU cables from the FSP VITA PSU. This provides maximum stability for the 12-core 5900X during heavy inference.

4 GPU Power

Each RX 7800 XT requires two 8-pin PCIe connections.

  • Cable Strategy: Use four separate PCIe cables from the PSU.
  • Safety: Avoid using "pigtail" cables (one cable splitting into two connectors) for these cards to prevent cable overheating and transient voltage drops during peak AI compute.

5 Case Headers & Fans

  • Lian Li Hub: Connect a SATA power cable to the fan/RGB hub on the back of the tray.
  • PWM Control: Connect the hub's 4-pin header to SYS_FAN1 on the motherboard.
  • JFP1: Connect power/reset switches. Note the "+" and "-" markings on the board.
  • USB: Connect the USB 3.0 (blue) and USB-C headers on the right edge.

6 BIOS Initialization

Once power is connected, boot into the BIOS (mash DEL).

  • XMP/A-XMP: Enable this to ensure your Patriot Viper RAM runs at its rated 3600MHz.
  • Resizable BAR: Ensure this is Enabled. It is mandatory for modern GPU performance and AI memory management.
  • PCIe Mode: Verify that the slots are running in Gen4 x8/x8 mode.
Final Step: Plug your monitor into the Top GPU (XFX). This will be your primary display output for both Windows and VR.
