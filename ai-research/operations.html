The Bi-Weekly Maintenance Loop

To prevent performance regressions (bit-rot) and ensure model freshness, follow this engineering loop every 14 days.

Step 1: Driver Sync

Update ROCm and the amdgpu kernel module. Check for new Vulkan/RADV optimizations in the Mesa drivers.

Step 2: Model Pruning

Delete experimental models that failed the "Aider Benchmark" to free up NVMe space for high-rank GGUF quants.

Step 3: Performance Re-Baseline

Run scripts/benchmark_ollama.py and compare results against the Visualizer baseline. If decode speeds drop >10%, rollback the recent driver update.

Agentic Guardrails

When running agents (Cline/Aider) locally, the primary failure mode is Context Overflow.

  • Ignore Strategy: Use .aiderignore to keep large binary files out of the LLM's context window.
  • VRAM Monitoring: Use rocm-smi to ensure your context hasn't spilled into system RAM.
