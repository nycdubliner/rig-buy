Domestic: Ireland

GG Machines IENEW
A highly reliable domestic system builder and parts retailer. They offer competitive pricing on heavy or fragile components that are expensive to ship internationally (like PC cases and power supplies). We sourced the 1000W ATX 3.1 PSU, Lancool 216 Case, and Xilence Cooler here.
Buying heavy items domestically avoids massive international shipping fees and ensures quick, easy returns if glass shatters in transit.
Elara.ie IENEW
A massive domestic retailer originally aimed at B2B but fully accessible to consumers. They have an exhaustive catalog of components.
While their baseline prices can sometimes skew higher, they are highly reliable for sourcing incredibly specific or niche workstation parts domestically.
MemoryC.ie IENEW
Based in Kildare, they are exceptional for smaller components. Originally focused heavily on RAM and storage media, they have expanded into full system components.
Excellent shipping speeds within Ireland. Highly recommended for grabbing a fast NVMe SSD or a specific RAM kit.
Adverts.ie / DoneDeal IEUSED
The primary Irish secondary market. This is where the real value is found if you are willing to negotiate. Sellers often overvalue their used parts initially, but cash offers and local pickup can drive prices down significantly.
We negotiated the XFX RX 7800 XT down to €400 here. Always test the hardware in person or request a video of it running a benchmark (like FurMark or 3DMark) before handing over cash.
CeX Ireland IEUSED
A massive physical and online presence for second-hand tech. While their GPU prices are often terrible (priced close to retail), their CPU prices can be surprisingly good.
We sourced the 12-core Ryzen 9 5900X here for €260. The massive advantage of CeX is their 24-month warranty on used hardware, which offers incredible peace of mind when buying complex silicon like a CPU.
Facebook Marketplace IEUSED
High risk, high reward. It lacks the feedback systems of Adverts or eBay, making it the Wild West. However, because it's so casual, you can occasionally find desperate sellers or incredible deals.
We secured the 64GB DDR4 Patriot Viper RAM kit here for €250. Never arrange shipping on FB Marketplace—face-to-face cash deals only to avoid scams.

International: European Union

eBay Germany (eBay.de) EUUSED
The holy grail for niche or high-end used PC components in Europe. Germany has a massive PC building culture, meaning their secondary market is flooded with flagship hardware that you simply cannot find in Ireland.
We sourced the extremely rare MSI MEG X570 Unify (essential for x8/x8 dual-GPU bifurcation) from eBay Germany. Buying from within the EU guarantees zero customs duties or surprise VAT bills upon delivery.
Amazon EU (.de / .fr / .es) EUNEW
When buying new components that Irish retailers lack, Amazon's European sites are the fallback. Amazon.de (Germany) typically has the best tech stock and pricing.
The Brexit Trap: Avoid Amazon.co.uk (UK) for anything over €150. Post-Brexit, high-value electronics coming from the UK will be hit with import duties and Irish VAT (23%) by customs, destroying any initial savings. Always stick to mainland EU domains.
Geizhals.eu EUNEW
An absolutely critical tool for EU builders. Geizhals is an aggregator that scrapes almost every hardware retailer across Germany, Austria, and Poland.
Use this strictly for price benchmarking. If a seller on Adverts.ie wants €500 for a used GPU, use Geizhals to prove that a brand new one from Germany costs €520. It is the ultimate negotiation weapon.
Paradigit / Caseking EUNEW
Large European retailers. Paradigit (Dutch) operates a localized .ie storefront which makes shipping easy, but their prices can be inflated. Caseking (German) has a legendary selection of watercooling and niche parts, but international shipping to Ireland can be steep (€20-€30).
Computeruniverse.net EUNEW
Another massive German retailer that is a staple for Irish builders. They offer flat-rate shipping to Ireland and have incredibly deep stock levels.
Often the best place to buy a brand new, current-generation GPU if you want to avoid the used market entirely, as their pricing regularly undercuts Irish domestic retailers even with shipping factored in.

International: Non-Optimized Classifieds

These sites are the "Adverts.ie" equivalents for the largest EU markets. Use these for deep-value hunting on GPUs and CPUs where local sellers may not be cross-referencing global eBay prices.

Subito.it (Italy)
Historically the best place for RTX 3090 bargains. Significant price gaps vs. eBay.
LeBonCoin (France)
High volume of localized PC builds. Excellent for finding 30-series GPUs.
Kleinanzeigen (Germany)
The largest localized market in the EU. High volume of flagship hardware.
Wallapop (Spain)
Great for Founders Edition cards and Corsair components.
OLX.pl (Poland)
Massive hardware market for Eastern/Central Europe. Note: Requires careful shipping negotiation.
