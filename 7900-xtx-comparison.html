Compatibility Advisory

Models marked with High Clearance are the only variants recommended for the Top Slot on standard ATX boards (like your MSI X570 Unify) when using a second card below. Using a 3.0-slot card in the top position will leave less than 5mm of clearance, likely leading to thermal throttling during long inference runs.
